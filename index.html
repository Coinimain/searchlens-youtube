MORE CONTROL

Go beyond basic search filters.

Use the controls you need, then let SearchLens clean and refine the results already in front of you.

📅

Custom date ranges

Search across specific years, months, or exact dates instead of relying only on broad presets.

🎯

Stricter relevance

Require words, match exact title phrases, and exclude terms that keep cluttering the results.

⏱️

Duration and views

Set minimum and maximum video lengths or narrow results by visible view counts.

📺

Channel controls

Focus on a channel name or exclude channels containing words you do not want.

🧹

Cleaner results

Hide Shorts, playlists, mixes, channel cards, live streams, shelves, and watched items.

↕

Sort loaded results

Reorder currently loaded matches by recency, views, or duration without leaving the page.

HOW IT WORKS

Search normally, then refine.

SearchLens adds a subtle launcher to YouTube search-result pages. Open it when you need more control, apply your filters, and keep browsing.

  1. 1
    Search

    Start from the extension popup or search YouTube normally.

  2. 2
    Refine

    Open SearchLens and choose the filters that matter.

  3. 3
    Explore

    Scroll for more results and SearchLens reapplies your local filters.
